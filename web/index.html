Managevity

Book your gym classes and get them into your own calendar. An independent, open-source client for gyms that use Sportivity.

Also on your computer

Not just a phone app: Managevity runs on Android, on Linux, and in any browser on any computer. Same features everywhere, with a layout that uses a wide screen.

Privacy friendly

No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no server of our own. The app talks only to your gym's Sportivity server and, if you turn it on, to your own calendar. Your password is kept in your device's keychain and is never stored in a browser. The source code is open for anyone to check.

Syncs to your calendar

Booked classes appear in your calendar and disappear when you cancel: straight to Nextcloud or any other CalDAV server, or to a calendar on your phone. With the trainer, the room, the address and an optional reminder.

And the rest

Timetable with filters, waiting lists, favourites, your class history, how busy the gym is, invoices as PDF, your details and ID photo, news. Works offline with what was loaded earlier. Dutch and English, light and dark.

Try it without an account

Sign in with username demo and password demo for a built-in demo with made-up data. Nothing is really booked.

In het Nederlands

Managevity is een gratis, opensource-app om je sportlessen te boeken bij sportscholen die Sportivity gebruiken, op je telefoon én op je computer. Geboekte lessen synchroniseren naar Nextcloud, een andere CalDAV-agenda of de agenda op je telefoon. Geen advertenties, geen tracking. Proberen kan met gebruikersnaam en wachtwoord demo.

Managevity is unofficial: it is not affiliated with or endorsed by b.o.s.s. or Sportivity. Free software under the GPL-3.0 licence.
